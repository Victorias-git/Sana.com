Questions, answered

Which flow is Regular Flow for?

Regular Flow is made for everyday days of your period, when your flow is neither very light nor very heavy.

How often should I change my pad?

Many people change every 4 to 6 hours, or sooner if the pad feels full or uncomfortable. Change more often if you notice any irritation.

How do I use the wings?

Peel the backing, press the pad centred in your underwear, then fold the wings around the edges and press them down so they stick to the outside.

How do I throw a used pad away?

Wrap it in its own wrapper or tissue and put it in a bin. Please don’t flush it.
